Why Choose Showrab Hospital?

We provide a fully integrated digital healthcare experience. Here is what you can do as a registered patient.

Consult Top Specialists

Search and book appointments with internationally trained doctors. Track your visit status in real-time from your personal dashboard.

Digital Lab Tests

Doctors can request lab tests, or you can self-book tests like Blood Work and MRI. View your detailed reports and doctor's remarks online.

Health Insurance Hub

Browse our exclusive insurance plans (e.g., Guardian Shurokkha, Child Protection). Check eligibility and purchase instantly to secure your family.

Emergency & ICU Access

In critical conditions, directly request an ICU bed from your dashboard, or call an ambulance with one click without logging in.

Centralized Medical History

Never lose a prescription. All your medical history, final lab reports with color-coded (Green/Red) doctor feedback are saved securely.

Smart AI Assistant

Have questions? Our 24/7 Chatbot is ready to guide you on how to book doctors, find lab results, or purchase insurance plans.
